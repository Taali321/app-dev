## Avatar: The Last Airbender (2005)

The original animated series follows **Aang**, the last Airbender, as he masters all four elements to stop the Fire Nation.

### Structure

1. **Book One: Water**
2. **Book Two: Earth**
3. **Book Three: Fire**

- **Total:** 3 books, 61 episodes
- **Creators:** Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko
- **Famous episode:** "The Tale of Iroh" :tea:

---

## The Legend of Korra (2012)

A sequel series set 70 years later, following **Korra**, the next Avatar, in a modern, industrial world.

| Book | Title | Main Villain |
| :--- | :---- | :----------- |
| Book One | Air | Amon |
| Book Two | Spirits | Unalaq |
| Book Three | Change | Zaheer |
| Book Four | Balance | Kuvira |

- **Total:** 4 books, 52 episodes
- Set in places like Republic City, with new tech such as *Satomobiles* and *pro-bending*

## Live-Action Movie (2010)

*The Last Airbender* was directed by M. Night Shyamalan and covers **Book One: Water**.

- ~~Fan favorite~~ Widely criticized by fans and critics
- Many fans prefer to forget it exists, and the Netflix series later became the "real" live-action version

## Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender (2026)

The first animated movie from **Avatar Studios**, continuing the story of the original series with an adult Aang.

- **Platform:** Paramount+ 
- **Premiere:** July 25, 2026
- **Directors:** Lauren Montgomery, Steve Ahn, William Mata
- **Voice cast:** Eric Nam (Aang), Steven Yeun (Zuko), Jessica Matten (Katara), Román Zaragoza (Sokka), Dionne Quan (Toph)
- **Story:** Aang learns of an ancient power that could save the Air Nomad culture from extinction

## Avatar: Seven Havens (2026)
